01 — The problem

Getting a garment made still takes dozens of people, hundreds of emails and months of waiting.

A brand with a good design and no sourcing team has two options: hire one, or trust an agent they can't see into. Both are slow. Both lose information between the sketch and the sewing line. Most small orders never get made at all.

02 — Fravash

A sourcing and production department you don't have to build.

You send a design. We source the fabric, cost the piece, develop the sample with you, place the order, check the quality and ship it to your warehouse. Quoting, planning, follow-up and records run on our own software; denim specialists check the decisions that matter.

  • SourcingFabric mill, sewing factory and laundry matched to the style. A clear price per piece, broken down.
  • DevelopmentFit, wash and trims approved on real garments. Tech pack, BOM, style sheet and size spec included.
  • ProductionOrder placed and followed step by step. Measurements, construction and shade checked before anything ships.
03 — How it runs

Software does the coordination. People check what matters.

  • SpeedQuotes and updates in hours, not weeks of email.
  • TransparencyEvery price, date and approval is recorded and visible to you, in one place.
  • ConsistencyWash and shade results are measured and logged, so the next order matches the last one.
  • Small ordersSoftware does the coordination, so a small run gets the same attention as a large one.
04 — Why denim first

We started in denim because it is the hardest thing to get right.

The fabric shrinks, the wash drifts, the fit lives or dies on half a centimetre. We have stood in the laundries and sat at the sewing lines. Denim and heavy woven, made in seven countries by factories we know by name.

05 — Where this goes

One request, and it gets made.

We start by running sourcing and production on software. The long-term goal is autonomous clothing manufacturing, including sewing, dyeing and washing, close to where the clothes are worn. Every order we run today teaches the system how to do the next one.

Send us a design.

A sketch, a tech pack or a reference garment. You'll have factory options and a price within two working days.
